# 2.10 Healthcare Safety Requirements

Hii ni sehemu muhimu sana kwa mfumo wetu wa afya.

## Hierarchy ya mfumo

```
Health Education
       ↓
Health Tracking
       ↓
Decision Support
       ↓
Clinical Care
```

Mfumo uko katika ngazi za juu za **Health Education, Health Tracking na Decision Support** (limited), na haujishughulishi kikamilifu na **Clinical Care** bila clinical governance.

## System inaweza (upported)

- ✅ Kukumbusha medication.
- ✅ Kuhifadhi taarifa za afya.
- ✅ Kuonyesha trends (kutoka patient-generated data).
- ✅ Kusaidia communication kati ya patient na verified professionals.
- ✅ Kutoa education.

## System haiwezi bila appropriate clinical governance

- ❌ Kufanya diagnosis.
- ❌ Kubadilisha dosage.
- ❌ Ku-prescribe dawa.
- ❌ Kudai emergency diagnosis.
- ❌ Kuchukua nafasi ya daktari.

## Safety principles
- AI / risk output sio medical diagnosis (FR-AI-006).
- Emergency triage na advice final hatujachukua; tuna-rwanya kwa professionals (BR-010, BR-011).
- Mfumo unatafsiriwa kama **support**, sio substitute kwa healthcare.
