# 2.2 User Requirements

## UR-01 — Patient

Patient aweze:

- Kutengeneza account.
- Kuingia kwenye mfumo.
- Kusimamia profile.
- Kusimamia medical profile.
- Kurekodi medication.
- Kupokea medication reminders.
- Kuweka clinic appointments.
- Kurekodi symptoms/pain.
- Kuona health timeline.
- Kuomba damu.
- Kuomba emergency assistance.
- Kuwasiliana na verified healthcare professionals.
- Kusoma health education.
- Kushiriki community.
- Kusimamia consent/access ya medical records.
- Kupokea notifications.

## UR-02 — Caregiver

Caregiver aweze:

- Kuunda account.
- Kuunganishwa na patient kwa authorization.
- Kuona taarifa alizopewa ruhusa.
- Kusaidia appointment management.
- Kupokea emergency notifications.
- Kusaidia medication tracking.

## UR-03 — Doctor

Doctor aweze:

- Kujiandikisha kama healthcare professional.
- Kuwasilisha taarifa za verification.
- Kusubiri admin verification.
- Kuonekana kama verified doctor baada ya approval.
- Kuwasiliana na patients waliomruhusu.
- Kuona permitted medical information.
- Kutoa clinical notes pale workflow inaruhusu.
- Kusimamia appointments zinazohusiana naye.

## UR-04 — Blood Donor

Donor aweze:

- Kujisajili.
- Kuweka blood group.
- Kuweka eneo.
- Kuweka last donation date.
- Kuweka availability.
- Kupokea eligible blood requests.
- Kukubali au kukataa request.

## UR-05 — Financial Donor

Aweze:

- Kuona verified campaigns.
- Kuchagua campaign.
- Kuchangia.
- Kuona transaction status.
- Kupata receipt.
- Kuona donation history.

## UR-06 — Administrator

Admin aweze:

- Kusimamia users.
- Kuhakiki doctors.
- Kuhakiki donors/workflows.
- Kuhakiki blood requests.
- Kusimamia emergency requests.
- Kuhakiki campaigns.
- Kusimamia payments.
- Kusimamia community.
- Kuona reports.
- Kuona audit logs.
- Kusimamia system configuration.

> Traceability: kila user requirement ina miadi ya functional requirements (FR) na acceptance criteria (AC).
