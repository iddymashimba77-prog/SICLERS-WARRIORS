## 1. Scope

Deliver the approved **patient-owned medication + symptom tracking** module:

- Medication regimen (FR-MED-001), schedules (FR-MED-002), dose logging with
  idempotency (FR-MED-004), dose history (FR-MED-005), adherence stats
  (FR-MED-006), and the health dashboard (FR-MED-007/health).
- Symptom journal (FR-SYM-001..005) with the approved read-only `symptom_types`
  vocabulary; pain scale 0..10 with crisis warning.
- Chronological health timeline (row 219 projection) for medication, doses and
  symptoms.
- Ownership/permission/audit gates only — cross-patient (doctor/caregiver)
  access remains behind the future consent stage (C2/C3).

NOT in scope (later stages): appointment module, push/FCM reminders
(AD-MD-9 open), consent graph, analytics (adherence computation offloaded),
medical-records corrections.

## 3. Data model (approved rows, quoted)

> `medications` (210): `uid`, `patient_id` FK, `name`, `generic_name`,
> `strength`, `dosage`, `route` CHECK, `frequency`, `start_date`, `end_date`
> null, `prescribed_by_id` FK null, `is_active`, `created_at`, `updated_at`,
> `deleted_at`.
> `medication_schedules` (211): `medication_id` FK, `time_of_day` time,
> `days_of_week` JSONB, `notes`.
> `medication_events` (212): `schedule_id` FK, `patient_id` FK, `status`
> CHECK(TAKEN MISSED SKIPPED SNOOZED), `taken_at` null, `note`, `created_at`
> (immutable; adherence derived).
> `symptoms` (218): `uid`, `patient_id` FK, `symptom_type_id` FK, `pain_level`
> SMALLINT CHECK 0..10, `triggers` JSONB, `duration_minutes`, `notes`,
> `recorded_at`, `created_at`.
> `health_timeline` (219): materialized projection (`patient_id`, `event_type`,
> `event_id`, `occurred_at`, `summary`, `payload` JSONB), indexed
> `(patient_id, occurred_at DESC)`.

Implementation notes:
- `route` vocabulary (ORAL/TOPICAL/INHALED/INJECTION/SUBLINGUAL/SUPPOSITORY/
  OTHER) matches the mobile design docs.
- `SymptomType` catalog: 27 approved entries seeded idempotently by `0003`
  (migration), with an `is_pain_related` marker (8 pain types) — the 0..10
  slider applies only to pain-related types.
- `medication_events.idempotency_key` nullable-UNIQUE supports the §30
  duplicate-key contract; `medevent_taken_at_ck` enforces
  `status='TAKEN' ⇒ taken_at is NOT NULL` at the DB.

| Endpoint | Method | Gate (own scope) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| `/api/medications/{uid}/log-dose` | POST | `medications.update` | Status + `taken_at` pairing; idempotent (409) |

| `/api/medications/stats` | GET | `medications.view` | 30-day adherence (FR-MED-006) |
| `/api/symptoms/` | GET/POST | `symptoms.view`/`symptoms.create` | Append-only |

All list endpoints use `CorePageNumberPagination` (page 20, `page_size`
overridable, max 100). `log-dose` returns the `DoseLogOutSerializer` event to
the client for immediate server-ack (MEDT-004 "server ack required").

## 5. Ownership, permission and audit (deny-by-default)

- `_patient_scope`: authenticated + `is_patient` + `account_status ∈ {ACTIVE,
  PENDING_VERIFICATION}` — a fresh registration may record its OWN health data
  pre-verification; professional cross-patient access is never reachable here.
- Querysets filter `patient=request.user` and omit soft-deleted rows — foreign
  uids resolve to 404, never 403/200 (IDOR-safe by construction).
- `MedicationAccess` maps method+action → allowed code; `SymptomAccess` for
  symptoms. `MedicationViewSet`/`SymptomViewSet` sit behind
  `IsAuthenticatedRespectStatus` + `IsPatient`.
- Every mutation writes a `SecurityEvent` with **event type only** (§21/§24);
  timeline `payload` carries non-clinical ids/labels (medication uid, status,
  pain level) — never free-text PHI.
